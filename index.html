🍷 Carter's Awesome Italian Sub Recipe 🍷

🥖 Ingredients

Bread & Base

  • 1 large, crusty Italian sub roll or hoagie (10–12 inches)
  • 1-2 tbsp olive oil & red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp dried oregano & flake salt

Meats & Cheeses (4–5 oz total)

  • 3 slices provolone cheese (aged or mild)
  • 1/4 lb Genoa salami, ultra-thinly sliced
  • 1/4 lb capicola (or ham/gabagool), ultra-thinly sliced
  • 1/4 lb mortadella (optional, for extra richness)

Toppings & Veggies

  • 1 cup shredded iceberg lettuce
  • 3-4 thin slices ripe tomato
  • Thinly sliced red onion
  • Pickled banana peppers or pickled cherry peppers

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Slice the sub roll open lengthwise, leaving the back edge intact as a hinge.
  2. Drizzle the inside of both halves with olive oil and red wine vinegar, then sprinkle with oregano and salt.
  3. Layer the provolone cheese flat along the bottom roll.
  4. Fold the thinly sliced salami, capicola, and mortadella onto the sandwich in loose waves to add volume.
  5. Top with shredded lettuce, tomato slices, red onion, and hot peppers.
  6. Close the sub, slice diagonally down the center, and enjoy!
